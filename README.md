# Smart Exoskeleton for Farmers using Piezoelectric Technology

## Overview

This project presents a wearable smart exoskeleton designed to assist farmers during physically demanding agricultural activities. The system integrates piezoelectric sensors, embedded control, and wearable mechanical support to reduce muscular strain while improving productivity and comfort.

The exoskeleton also harvests energy generated from human movement using piezoelectric materials, enabling low-power applications such as sensor operation and LED indicators.

## Working Principle

### Step 1

The farmer wears the exoskeleton.

### Step 2

Piezoelectric sensors generate electrical energy from body movements.

### Step 3

The microcontroller processes sensor inputs.

### Step 4

The actuator provides mechanical support during lifting and bending.

### Step 5

Harvested energy powers low-power electronic components such as indicators or sensors.
